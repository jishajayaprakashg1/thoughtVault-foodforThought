### Personal Branding notes:

Praising ourselves is difficult. We need to identify why we want to do personal branding:
is it for a new job, is it for fame, is it for wealth, is it for learning, etc.

*"Change is the only constant."*

*It is not about "Survival of the fittest" anymore it is about "Survival of the BEST".*

*"Be an opprtunist."*

To bring a change in your personality: introduce small discomforts in daily life: 
Example: drink green tea instead of milk tea, sleep on the other side of the bed.

### What is your personal brand?
- Skills
- Personality
- Experience
- Expertise
- Values
- Strengths

### Start with : 
*Self Discovery: what is your IKIGAI, what makes you unique and what is your SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats*

Examples of Strengths:
- Solve complex problems
- Easy to motivate myself to achieve goals
- Good at understanding and empathising with others
- Staying calm under pressure
- Skilled at planning and organising tasks

Examples of Values:
- Prioritise honesty and integrity in my interactions.
- Value teamwork and collaboration.
- Continuous learning and personal growth is important to contribute to my community
- Balanced approach in work and life 
- Value time: Coming to office on time, accepting and attending meetings on time
- Stay true to commitments
- empathetic

### Create a compelling narrative to tell your story and highlight the uniqueness you bring:
+ Who are you?
+ What do you do?
+ Who do you do it for?
+ The transformation you create?

### 3C's of personal branding:
1. Clarity
2. Consistency
3. Communication: Content, Confidence, Connection

### Online and offline presence : 
Tip: Establish yourself as a thought leader by sharing valuable insights and expertise.
Contribute by speaking at conferences, participating in panel discussions and showcasing your knowledge
Engage with audience
* Respond, not reaction
* Showcasing your knowledge

### Be an opportunist:
* Elevator pitch:
  - Who you are? JJ 
  - What is your role and where are you working? Lead Engg at ___
  - Explain about the location of the company or what is does ? ecommerce giant in south America predominantly Chile
  - Connection : I have read about your testing strategy and courses?
  - Ask : Can you be my mentor, how can i apply?
  - Close : Stay in touch i will write to you ?

### Action Plan: 
*Personal branding will take time.*
- Step1: Identify your strengths and values.
- Step2: Current brand audit and redefine your brand.
- Step3: Set SMART goals.
- Step4: Ensure your Online and offline presence.
